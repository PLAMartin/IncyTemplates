# 0010 — "Proven–Better–New Assessment" appearing in two lists

## Status
Assumption — needs product-owner confirmation

## Context
Spec §37 lists "Proven–Better–New Assessment" as both a free standalone
product and a component of the paid "Idea Validation Pack" bundle. It's
ambiguous whether that's the same free item bundled for convenience, or a
distinct paid edition with the same underlying method.

## Decision (assumption, pending confirmation)
Treated as two distinct products: the free standalone version, and a
separate paid edition (different slug) inside the bundle. Bundles don't
re-list an already-free product as one of their paid components.

## Follow-up
Confirm the intended relationship with the product owner; trivial to merge
back into one product if the assumption is wrong.
