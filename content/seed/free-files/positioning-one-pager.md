## Objective

A one-page positioning statement — built from an action/outcome/admiration pairing — plus a chosen cut-through tactic, written down and ready to test on real prospective customers.

## Intended user

Founders who've named the problem they solve and now need to decide how to present the product so it actually gets noticed.

## Situation

Pair the action your ideal customer takes with your product to the outcome they get, tie it to what they admire if there's a natural connection, and pick one of five cut-through tactics — scary, strange, sexy, free gifts or familiar — that's realistic for your situation.

Use once you know your target customer and the core outcome you deliver, before you finalise a name or launch plan.
