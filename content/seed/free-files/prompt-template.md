## Situation

Before asking a chatbot for real help with a specific task, work through CARE: Context (who or what it should act as), Action (what it should do), Result (the format or output you want), and an optional Example.

## Process

1. Context — who or what should the chatbot act as, and what's the situation?
2. Action — what should the chatbot actually do?
3. Result — what format or output do you want back?
4. Example — a model to guide the style, if you have one (optional).
5. Decide whether to add: "Now ask me one question at a time, waiting for my answer in between, to help me think this through."

Three ready-made patterns to reuse directly:

- Explain [topic] simply, with 2 real-world examples.
- Generate [n] ideas for [task], each tailored to [audience].
- Compare [option A] vs [option B] by [criteria], in table form.

Expected to take roughly 5–10 minutes per prompt.
