## Objective

A completed prep sheet for one specific upcoming negotiation, covering three tactics: your fallback, your opening anchor, and your alternative offers.

## Intended user

Founders and small teams heading into a specific negotiation — pricing, hiring, a contract, a partnership.

## Situation

Before any negotiation you care about the outcome of, work through it tactic by tactic: decide your fallback if no deal happens (BATNA), decide the first number or position you'll open with (anchor), and prepare two or three alternative offers you'd be equally happy with (MESOs).

Use before sitting down to negotiate anything — pricing, hiring, a contract, a partnership.

## Process

1. Name the specific negotiation — what's being discussed, and with whom.
2. Decide your fallback (BATNA) — what you'll do if no deal gets done.
3. Decide your anchor — the first number or position you'll put on the table.
4. Prepare two or three alternative offers (MESOs) — each acceptable to you, each trading off differently.
5. Re-read all three before you sit down.

Expected to take roughly 5–10 minutes per negotiation.
