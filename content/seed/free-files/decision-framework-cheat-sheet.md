## Objective

A quick-reference comparison of four thinking frameworks — Six Thinking Hats, First Principles, Razors and the Boundary Rule — so you can scan it the moment a decision shows up and pick the one that fits.

## Intended user

Founders facing a lot of differently-shaped everyday decisions who want a quick reference rather than a deep process for each one.

## Situation

Compare the four frameworks against who's involved, the shape of the choice, whether there's a precedent to copy, and how much time the decision is actually worth — then apply the one that fits.

Use in the moment a decision shows up, not as a one-off exercise.
