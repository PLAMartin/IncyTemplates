## Objective

A running log for capturing ideas daily, tagged by source, and reviewed on a regular cadence rather than judged the moment they're written down.

## Intended user

Founders and would-be founders building a repeatable idea-generation habit, before they have a specific idea to assess.

## Situation

Log one idea at a time as it occurs — tagged as scratch-your-own-itch, address-a-niche or improve-existing — without filtering as you go. Review the log weekly, not daily, and pull out whichever entries still feel interesting once the initial excitement has worn off.
