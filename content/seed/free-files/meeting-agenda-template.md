## Objective

A documented meeting agenda — purpose, outcomes, process, participants and time allocation — completed before the meeting happens.

## Intended user

Founders and small teams who want to plan a meeting properly rather than sending an invite with just a title.

## Situation

Start with a clear, unique title and purpose. Define the supporting outcomes the meeting needs to reach, then the process, participants and time allocation for each. Send the agenda out ahead of time.

Use whenever you're organising a meeting that involves more than one or two people.
