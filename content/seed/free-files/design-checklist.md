## Objective

A completed design review of a specific product or screen, checked against Dieter Rams' ten principles of good design.

## Intended user

Founders and small teams designing their own product without a dedicated designer to catch what they can't see themselves.

## Situation

Before shipping any meaningful change to a product's design, check it against all ten principles: innovative, useful, aesthetic, understandable, unobtrusive, honest, long-lasting, thorough, environmentally friendly, and as little design as possible.

## Process

1. Innovative — does it take a genuinely original approach, grounded in what's actually possible today?
2. Useful — does every part of it help someone actually use the product?
3. Aesthetic — is it well executed, not just decorated?
4. Understandable — is it close to self-explanatory?
5. Unobtrusive — does the design stay out of the way, rather than drawing attention to itself?
6. Honest — does it avoid overstating what the product can actually do?
7. Long-lasting — would it still look right in five years?
8. Thorough — has the small stuff been cared for, not left arbitrary?
9. Environmentally friendly — is it lean and efficient, without unnecessary clutter?
10. As little design as possible — is it cut down to the essentials?

Expected to take roughly 5–10 minutes per product or screen.
