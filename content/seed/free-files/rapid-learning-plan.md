## Objective

A completed four-step learning plan for one specific skill, covering Deconstruction, Selection, Sequencing and Stakes.

## Intended user

Founders and small teams picking up a new skill — a tool, a technique, a domain — that they need to get good at quickly.

## Situation

Once you've picked a specific skill to learn, plan it against all four DSSS steps before you start putting in hours: break it into parts (Deconstruction), find the highest-leverage 20% (Selection), decide the order to learn them in (Sequencing), and build in real accountability (Stakes).

Use before you start, not partway through.

## Process

1. Name the specific skill you're learning.
2. Deconstruct it into its smaller, independent parts.
3. Select the highest-leverage 20% of those parts.
4. Sequence the order you'll learn them in.
5. Define the stakes that will keep you going once motivation fades.

Expected to take roughly 5–10 minutes per skill.
