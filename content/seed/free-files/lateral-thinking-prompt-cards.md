## Objective

Five lateral thinking technique cards — perceptual change, random input, provocation, specificity, scale — usable as a physical or written deck whenever you're stuck on a problem.

## Intended user

Founders who want a quick, repeatable way to approach a stuck problem from a different angle, without needing a screen.

## Situation

Pick one card, apply its prompt to the problem you're stuck on, and write down whatever it sparks without judging it yet. Work through more than one card before comparing results.

Use whenever you're stuck, not as a one-off exercise.
