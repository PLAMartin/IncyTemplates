## Objective

A prioritised task list, sorted first by importance and urgency, then by value divided by duration for whatever's left.

## Intended user

Founders and small teams with more tasks than time who want a documented, repeatable way to decide what to work on next.

## Situation

Sort tasks into the Eisenhower quadrants (important/urgent) first, then apply Weighted Processing Time scoring — value divided by duration — to rank what's left within each quadrant.

Use whenever your task list has grown past what you can hold in your head, or when you're not confident you're working on the right thing.
