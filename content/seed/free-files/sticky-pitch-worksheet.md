## Objective

A completed factor-by-factor check of one specific pitch or message, covering ten factors: Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Story, Social Currency, Triggers, Public, Practical Value.

## Intended user

Founders and small teams about to pitch an idea, product or feature to customers, investors or their own team.

## Situation

Once you have a specific pitch or message drafted, work through it factor by factor: does it stick (Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Story) and does it spread (Social Currency, Triggers, Public, Practical Value)?

Use before delivering the pitch for real — this checks a pitch, it doesn't write one from scratch.

## Process

1. Write out the pitch or message as it currently stands.
2. Check it against each of the six "stick" factors — Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Story.
3. Check it against each of the four "spread" factors — Social Currency, Triggers, Public, Practical Value.
4. Note which factors are missing, and revise the pitch to address the first one.
5. Re-check before delivering the pitch for real.

Expected to take roughly 5–10 minutes per pitch.
